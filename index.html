Concept 4: Depth Lock

The exact Apple iOS wallpaper depth effect. 4-layer spatial sandwich where the depth-cut foreground subject occludes the giant clock, center lyrics, and transport controls with blurred halos.

  • Subject layers OVER lyrics & clock
  • Frosted blur halo surrounding buttons
  • Full-bleed immersive background
  • Zero dashboard cards or sidebars
